What "share" means
If 200 serious miners split the testnet evenly, each holds ~0.5%. Running more/bigger GPUs raises your share; the lane is a race for a fixed pie. Same logic for agents (by spend) and validators (by stake & uptime).

It's proportional

Every number scales with total network participation, which nobody knows yet. Treat these as your share of a bucket, not a promise.

The miner lane is the biggest airdrop bucket and the majority of long-term supply and 85% of every inference fee. Its real value dwarfs the genesis slice.
